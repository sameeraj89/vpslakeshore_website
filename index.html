Innovation & Firsts

Breaking barriers
in medicine

VPS Lakeshore has established national and world firsts in surgical technique — consistently bringing the most advanced treatments to patients in Kerala and across India.

  • World's first endo-robotic surgery for post-cricoid cancer (2023)
  • India's first minimally invasive bunion correction surgery (2024)
  • India's first Centre for Complex Head & Neck Cancer
  • Guinness World Record — Largest Model of a Human Organ
  • First Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy performed in Kerala (Dr. H. Ramesh)

Our First 20+ Years

VPS Lakeshore opened its doors in 2003, bringing quaternary care to Kerala for the first time. From groundbreaking science to compassionate care, VPS Lakeshore has spent two decades improving lives and pushing the boundaries of what's possible.

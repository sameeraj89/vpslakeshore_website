Patient Care

Care that puts the
patient first

From your first consultation to post-treatment follow-up, our team coordinates care across specialties. Our multilingual coordinators support patients at every step — from 40+ countries.

  • Dedicated International Patient Services (ips@lakeshorehospital.org)
  • Medical visa invitation & travel coordination
  • Arabic, Hindi, Malayalam, English & more
  • Cashless insurance processing, all major insurers
  • Air, water & surface ambulance — listed in Limca Book of Records

Innovation & Firsts

Breaking barriers
in medicine

VPS Lakeshore has established national and world firsts in surgical technique — consistently bringing the most advanced treatments to patients in Kerala and across India.

  • World's first endo-robotic surgery for post-cricoid cancer (2023)
  • India's first minimally invasive bunion correction surgery (2024)
  • India's first Centre for Complex Head & Neck Cancer
  • Guinness World Record — Largest Model of a Human Organ
  • First Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy performed in Kerala (Dr. H. Ramesh)

World First · Surgery
World's first endo-robotic surgery for post-cricoid cancer performed at VPS Lakeshore, Kochi
A pioneering procedure removed a tumour extending from the head and neck into the oesophagus using robotic assistance through endoscopic access — restoring the 75-year-old patient's ability to speak and swallow.

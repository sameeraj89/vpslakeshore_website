Hospital Profile

One of Kerala's largest
quaternary care centres

VPS Lakeshore is recognised as a leader in medical education, groundbreaking research, and innovative, patient-centred clinical care — the hospital of choice for patients worldwide.

Incorporated as a public limited company in 1996 and officially opening its doors to integrated healthcare in January 2003, VPS Lakeshore has emerged as a centre of international healthcare excellence through a broad range of advanced clinical programmes, cutting-edge treatments and comprehensive diagnostic services.

The hospital stands with 470 beds, more than 35 specialties, and is a centre of excellence for multi-organ transplant services — one of the largest quaternary care hospitals in India. A new block will add a further 250 inpatient beds, a comprehensive radiation oncology wing and family-friendly patient rooms.

VPS Lakeshore is part of VPS Healthcare, a global healthcare group operating across 9 countries including UAE, UK, Germany and India — with flagship properties Burjeel and Medeor.

Message from the Chairman

"The greatest wealth
is good health."

"It is health that is real wealth and not pieces of gold and silver." — Mahatma Gandhi

When we started our first hospital in 2007, our focus was on delivering quality healthcare to areas deprived of basic healthcare services. Today, with operations across the Middle East, Europe, UK and India, we still strive to deliver the best of care and wellness to people across the world.

With VPS Lakeshore, the bedrock of our global healthcare mission has been set solid in God's Own Country. For more than two decades, VPS Lakeshore has been taking giant strides in delivering premium care and path-breaking innovation in the healthcare arena.

The hospital stands with 470 beds, more than 40 specialities and a centre of excellence for multi-organ transplant services — one of the largest quaternary care hospitals in India. We are glad that today we have VPS Lakeshore Hospital as part of our global healthcare mission and together we look forward to providing quality healthcare services and shaping a healthier future.

Managing Director's Message

Extraordinary doctors.
Extraordinary care.

VPS Lakeshore is one of only a few hospitals named among Kerala's top performers on key quality measures by the leading accreditors of healthcare organisations in India.

In keeping with our 20-year heritage, everyone on our team — from our medical, nursing, paramedical and supportive service healthcare professionals to the board of directors — is working together to ensure that quality healthcare is available to our patients. Our team of extraordinary doctors provides extraordinary care, on a foundation of innovation, compassion, and service excellence.

VPS Lakeshore is a 470-bedded, quaternary care multi super specialty hospital best known for management of digestive diseases and as an oncology centre which pioneered peripheral stem cell therapy in adults and children. We are continually investing in and developing new services and programmes that keep our system at the very forefront of medicine.

Our goal is that every patient and family member has a positive experience in our facility. We look forward to being your hospital of choice for all of your future healthcare needs.

Our Purpose

Vision, Mission
& Quality Policy

Vision
To be the destination of choice for people seeking superior healthcare through leading-edge treatments and research.
Mission
To deliver world-class, patient-centred healthcare with compassion and integrity — making advanced medicine accessible to all strata of society.
Quality Policy
VPS Lakeshore is committed to providing safe and quality patient care by meeting national and international standards.
  • Compliance with NABH standards and accreditation requirements
  • Continual improvement in clinical outcomes and patient safety
  • Evidence-based clinical practice across all departments
  • Investing in staff training, research and technology
  • Transparent, patient-first governance and ethical practice
  • Bureau Veritas certified · NABH accredited · Green OT certified
Milestones

A history of
firsts

2003
Hospital Opens
VPS Lakeshore officially opens in Kochi as a multi-specialty hospital following incorporation in 1996.
2004
Limca Book of Records
Air, water and surface ambulance services listed — a first in India — in the Limca Book of Records.
2016
VPS Healthcare Acquisition
VPS Healthcare acquires majority stake, integrating the hospital into its global network and accelerating expansion.
2019
Guinness World Record
VPS Lakeshore's 'HEART' — a full-scale anatomical replica — awarded Guinness World Record for Largest Model of a Human Organ.
Dec 2023
World First: Endo-Robotic Surgery
World's first endo-robotic surgery for post-cricoid cancer — restoring speech and swallowing in a 75-year-old patient.
Jan 2024
India First: Bunion Surgery
India's first minimally invasive bunion (hallux valgus) correction using small incisions and specialised instruments.
Oct 2024
India First: Head & Neck Cancer Centre
Inaugurated India's first dedicated Centre for Complex Head & Neck Cancer, integrating surgery, radiation and medical oncology.
Nov 2025
Asia First: Nanoscopic ACL Surgery
Asia's first nanoscopic ACL reconstruction and meniscal repair — a breakthrough for sportspersons and knee injury patients.
Jan 2026
Rare 3D Laparoscopic Kidney Removal
Rare 3D laparoscopic removal of a 4 kg giant polycystic kidney — showcasing the depth of VPS Lakeshore's MIS capabilities.

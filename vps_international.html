International Patient Services

Everything arranged.
You focus on healing.

📩
Medical Second Opinion
Send your records and scans electronically. A senior consultant reviews your case and provides a written opinion within 48–72 hours — before you travel.
📄
Medical Visa Support
We provide official invitation letters, cost estimates and supporting documents required for your Indian medical visa application from your embassy.
🚗
Airport Transfers
Complimentary pickup from Cochin International Airport (COK) arranged for patients. Kochi is well connected with direct flights from the Gulf, Europe and major Indian cities.
🏨
Accommodation
Partner hotels and service apartments within 5 km of the hospital for patients and families. Special rates negotiated for long-stay patients.
💳
Insurance & Cashless Billing
We process claims directly with major international insurers. Our billing team provides itemised, transparent statements. Insurance desk: insurancehelpdesk@lakeshorehospital.org · +91-484-2772067
📲
Telemedicine Follow-up
After returning home, continue consultations with your treating specialist via our video consultation platform — no need to travel for routine follow-ups.

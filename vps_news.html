World's first endo-robotic surgery for post-cricoid cancer performed at VPS Lakeshore, Kochi
A pioneering procedure removed a tumour extending from the head and neck region into the oesophagus using robotic assistance through endoscopic access — restoring the 75-year-old patient's ability to speak and swallow. First-ever such procedure performed globally.

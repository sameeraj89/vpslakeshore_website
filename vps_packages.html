Lakeshore Basic Health Plan

Complete health
screening in one day

The package simplifies health check-up for you. Arrive at 7:30 AM on an empty stomach. Our PR Officer will guide you through all investigations, after which a complimentary breakfast is served.

How It WorksMake a prior appointment by calling 0484-2701032 / 2701033. Arrive at 7:30 AM on an empty stomach. Once investigations are complete, breakfast is served. Your physician will evaluate results and advise you. If any health concerns are identified, you will be referred to the relevant department.

To book a health package or for any enquiries, call our Public Relations team.

Lakeshore Women Health Plan · Above 40

Comprehensive care
for women above 40

Women above 40 should undergo a thorough clinical check every year. This plan screens for anaemia, cancer (ovary, cervix, breast), uterine fibroids, osteoporosis and menopausal syndrome. Correct diet and regular physical exercise are keys to good health.

Additional Consultations IncludedClinical Psychologist · Senior Dietician · Physiotherapist

VPS Lakeshore Ayurkshethra

Kerala's healing
heritage, integrated

VPS Lakeshore Ayurkshethra integrates classical Ayurvedic treatments with modern diagnostic support — offering Kerala's ancient healing tradition within a contemporary hospital environment.

Treatments include Panchakarma, Rasayana therapies, joint care programmes and post-surgical rehabilitation using traditional methods. All treatments are performed by trained Ayurvedic physicians.
